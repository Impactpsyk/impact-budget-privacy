Privacy Policy for Impact Budget

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Impact Budget is a personal budgeting app developed by Impact Group. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information We Collect

Impact Budget is designed to store your budgeting information locally on your device. The app may allow you to enter information such as wallets, income, expenses, categories, notes, and related budget records.

The current version of Impact Budget does not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a server that receives or stores your personal budget entries.

Local Data Storage

Budget data entered into the app is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, clear app data, reset your device, or delete your local data, that information may be removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

Impact Budget may be distributed through Google Play. Google Play and Android may collect certain information according to Google’s own privacy policies, including installation, device, crash, and diagnostic information.

If future versions of the app include features such as advertisements, analytics, cloud sync, account login, or in-app purchases, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those features before or when they are introduced.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal budgeting data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Impact Budget is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

Because budget data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data by deleting records inside the app, clearing the app’s data from your device settings, or uninstalling the app.
